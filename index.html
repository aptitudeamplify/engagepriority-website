How EngagePriority Enforces Lead Response

From new inbound lead to confirmed action, EngagePriority tracks the response cycle and escalates when action stalls.

1

Lead Received

When a new online lead reaches an approved connected source, EngagePriority receives the payload and starts the response-enforcement cycle.

Lead status: Received
2

Agent Assigned

EngagePriority assigns the lead using your configured routing rules and records who owns the active response cycle.

Owner: Assigned
3

Response Enforced

The assigned agent receives one secure SMS link. If action is not confirmed, EngagePriority continues reminders, reassignment, or escalation according to the configured workflow.

Response status: Action confirmed
